## About me

I turn raw, fragmented data into trustworthy reporting, useful dashboards, and practical recommendations. My work spans the full analytics lifecycle—from defining the question and validating source data to modeling metrics, communicating findings, and enabling better decisions.

- 📊 Focused on business intelligence, product analytics, and performance reporting
- 🔍 Comfortable with exploratory analysis, KPI design, data quality, and root-cause investigation
- 🧩 Interested in building repeatable workflows that make analysis easier to trust and reuse
- 🤝 Open to collaborating on data-led products, dashboards, and analytical case studies

| Area | How I approach it |
| --- | --- |
| Business framing | Translate broad questions into measurable decisions and success criteria |
| Data quality | Check completeness, consistency, grain, definitions, and edge cases before analysis |
| Analysis | Use descriptive, diagnostic, and segmented analysis to explain what changed and why |
| BI & reporting | Build clear KPI views with useful comparisons, filters, and documented definitions |
